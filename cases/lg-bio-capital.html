Live client project

LG Bio Capital Partners

A website for a life science advisory company where the main goal was to make a complex professional field feel calm, credible and easy to understand.

View live site Contact me

Context

A live client project for a life science advisory company working with specialist, credibility-sensitive content.

Client requirements

Create a professional online presence where companies, investors and partners can understand the advisory offer without the content feeling heavy.

My contribution

Web design, frontend and content structure. I focused on layout, hierarchy, navigation and making the content easier to follow.

Delivered result

A responsive website presenting the company, advisory areas, approach, news, experience and contact route.

Process

Making complex content feel calmer

1. Structure first

I started by looking at what a visitor needs first: what the company does, who it helps and why it is credible. That helped shape a clearer page order.

2. Visual direction

I kept the design calm and professional with generous whitespace, a restrained color direction and a hero section that feels more advisory than promotional.

3. Frontend implementation

The layout was built to work across screen sizes, with a strong hero, readable content sections and navigation that stays simple.

Design and development

From visual hierarchy to frontend

Design decisions

I used a calm visual hierarchy because the subject is already complex. The design should support trust and clarity, not compete with the content.

Frontend implementation

I kept the HTML and CSS structure straightforward so the layout remains responsive, readable and easier to maintain without a framework.

Testing and iteration

I checked the site visually across screen sizes and adjusted spacing, image handling and hierarchy. A formal usability test would be a useful next step.

Challenges

Balancing credibility with clarity

Specialist content

The subject needed enough detail to feel credible, while the page still had to be understandable to someone scanning it for the first time.

Professional tone

The visual direction had to support trust without making the site feel generic or overly promotional.

Evidence limits

I can document the live implementation and responsive hierarchy, but I have not added conversion claims or client metrics because those have not been measured.

Final result

A live client website with a clear hierarchy

The delivered website presents the company's offer, approach, professional activity and experience in a responsive structure. It demonstrates how I translate real client material into a calmer frontend presentation.

Reflection: Content hierarchy matters most when the subject is already complex. The interface needs to reduce effort without making the company sound less substantial.

Next step: I would add documented task-based testing and before-and-after content examples if I continue developing this case.
