Live client project

LG Bio Capital Partners

A professional advisory website designed to make specialist life-science and investment content feel calm, credible and easy to follow.

View live site

Problem

Specialist content needed a clear hierarchy without losing professional credibility.

Target users

Life-science companies, investors, boards and professional partners.

My contribution

Web design, frontend implementation and content structure.

Technologies

HTML, CSS, responsive design, content hierarchy

01

Client context and requirements

The company needed a professional online presence where visitors could understand the advisory offer without the subject feeling heavy or overly promotional.

Core requirement

Explain what the company does, who it helps and why the experience is relevant.

Content constraint

Keep enough specialist detail to feel credible while making the first scan approachable.

02

Design direction

I used generous whitespace, restrained colour and a clear editorial hierarchy so the visual design supports trust instead of competing with the content.

Clear hierarchy

The advisory offer appears before detailed services and experience.

Professional tone

Photography, typography and spacing create a calm advisory character.

Simple navigation

The page order moves from offer to services, approach, proof and contact.

Responsive reading

Content, imagery and actions stay understandable on smaller screens.

03

Implementation

The website was built with a straightforward HTML and CSS structure so the layout remains responsive, readable and maintainable without a framework.

01

Offer

The hero establishes strategic advisory as the primary message.

02

Services

Advisory areas add detail after the initial positioning.

03

Proof

News and experience support the professional story.

04

Contact

The contact route appears after enough context has been provided.

04

Delivered result and reflection

The live website presents the offer, approach, professional activity and experience in a responsive structure. No conversion claims or client metrics are included because they have not been measured.

What worked well

A calmer hierarchy makes complex content easier to scan without reducing its substance.

Biggest challenge

Balancing specialist credibility with an approachable first impression.

What I would improve

Add documented task-based testing and before-and-after content examples.
